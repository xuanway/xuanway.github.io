About

I am currently an Assistant Professor at the Faculty of Computility Microelectronics (CME), Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology (SUAT). Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the AI Chip Center for Emerging Smart Systems (ACCESS), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I worked with Prof. Zhongrui Wang & Prof. Tsung-Yi Ho. I received my Ph.D. from the Institute of Computing Technology (ICT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) , under the supervision of Prof. Zhimin Tang & Prof. Dongrui Fan, and my bachelor's degree from Northeastern University (NEU), Shenyang, China. My work has received the Best Paper Runner Up Award at SRDS 2024 and the Best Paper Award at SEKE 2021. In academic service, I have served as a Technical Program Committee (TPC) member for ICCD 2026 and ASP-DAC 2027, an appointed Session Chair for the 44th IEEE International Conference on Computer Design (ICCD 2026), an Artifact Evaluation (AE) Committee member for MICRO 2026, and a reviewer for IEEE TIFS and ACM TKDD.

My research aims to advance AI computility through high-performance, energy-efficient, and trustworthy AI computing. To achieve this goal, I focus on novel AI accelerator architectures, AI compiler optimization, and hardware security, with an emphasis on cross-layer co-design and optimization across computing architectures, compilers, and security mechanisms. Through this integrated approach, I seek to develop AI computing systems with enhanced computational performance, energy efficiency, and security and trustworthiness.
